Understanding the Go Board

Before playing Go, let's understand the board, the stones, and where each move is placed.

01

The Go Board

A standard Go board has 19 horizontal and 19 vertical lines, forming 361 intersections.

02

Black and White Stones

Two players use black and white stones. Black traditionally makes the first move.

03

Intersections and Star Points

Stones are placed on intersections rather than inside the squares. Star points are marked positions that help players recognize important areas of the board.
