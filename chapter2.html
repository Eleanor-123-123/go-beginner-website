LESSON 01

Discover Liberties

Every stone needs liberties to remain on the board. Explore the board and observe how empty intersections determine a stone's freedom.

How to explore:

Select an intersection to place a stone. Observe the highlighted empty intersections around the stone.

Select an intersection to place a stone.
Remember: A stone's liberties are the empty intersections directly connected to it horizontally or vertically. Diagonal intersections do not count.

Important Rule: When a stone or connected group has no remaining liberties, it is captured and removed from the board.
CHAPTER SUMMARY

What Have You Learned?

You now understand the relationship between stones, liberties, connected groups, and capturing.

  • Liberties are empty adjacent intersections.
  • Connected stones share their liberties.
  • A group with no liberties is captured.
