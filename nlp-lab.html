UNDER THE HOOD

A small baseline.
A visible reasoning trail.

This prototype favors transparency over pretending to be a trained model. Every classification can be inspected and explained.

01

Normalize

Lowercase Latin text, remove punctuation, and collapse repeated spaces while preserving Chinese characters.

02

Weight Signals

Domain phrases and keywords contribute different scores to six Go-learning intent categories.

03

Route Learners

The highest-scoring intent produces an explanation and links to the most relevant chapter.

BASELINE LIMITATIONS

No training data, embeddings, or semantic model are used. Ambiguous wording and unseen synonyms may fall back to “Uncertain.” A future version could replace the lexicon with a supervised classifier while preserving this interface as an interpretable baseline.
