CHAPTER 02

Understanding Liberties and Capturing

Learn how stones survive, connect, and capture opposing stones.

What is a Liberty?

A liberty is an empty intersection directly connected to a stone horizontally or vertically.

What is Capturing?

When a stone or connected group loses all its liberties, it is captured and removed from the board.

Rules in This Practice Game

  • Black plays first.
  • Players take turns placing one stone.
  • Captured groups are automatically removed.
  • Suicide moves are not allowed.
  • The Ko rule prevents immediate repetition.
  • Two consecutive passes end the game.
