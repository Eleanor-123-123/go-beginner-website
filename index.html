Chapter 1: Understanding Go

1. The Go Board

A standard Go board consists of 19 horizontal lines and 19 vertical lines, forming 361 intersections.

2. Go Stones

Go is played with two types of stones: black and white. Black plays first, and players take turns placing stones on the intersections.

3. Intersections

Stones must be placed on the intersections of the horizontal and vertical lines, not inside the squares.

Chapter 2: Basic Rules of Go

1. Liberties

A liberty is an empty intersection directly adjacent to a stone or a connected group of stones.

2. Capturing Stones

When a stone or a connected group of stones has no remaining liberties, it is captured and removed from the board.

3. Suicide Rule

A player generally cannot make a move that leaves their own group with no liberties, unless the move captures opposing stones.

4. The Ko Rule

The Ko rule prevents players from immediately repeating a previous board position.

5. Scoring and Winning

Players compete to control territory on the board. The winner is determined by the scoring rules used in the game.
