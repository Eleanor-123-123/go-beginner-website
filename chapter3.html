CHAPTER 03

Play Go Yourself

You have learned the basics of Go. Now it is time to put your knowledge into practice and play your first game.

BEFORE YOU PLAY

Three Things to Remember

Keep these three basic principles in mind before making your first move.

01

Take Turns

Black plays first. Players take turns placing one stone on an empty intersection.

02

Keep Your Liberties

Every group of stones needs at least one liberty to remain alive on the board.

03

Capture Your Opponent

When an opponent's connected group has no liberties, it is captured and removed.

YOUR FIRST CHALLENGE

How to Practice

Follow these simple steps to become familiar with playing Go.

01

Start with Black

Black makes the first move. Choose an empty intersection and place your first stone.

02

Think About Liberties

Before placing a stone, consider the surrounding empty intersections.

03

Try to Capture Stones

Look for opponent groups with very few liberties and learn how captures happen.

04

Play Again and Improve

Restart the board and try different moves. Learn from every game.
